Our Heritage

The Art of Mitti

Where every piece tells a story of earth, fire, and human spirit

100+ Years of Legacy

A Tradition Passed Through Generations

Deep in the heart of India, where the soil is rich with clay and the air carries the scent of wood-fired kilns, our artisans have been shaping earth into art for generations. Each vessel we create is more than utility — it's a prayer to the earth, a conversation between fire and form.

Our potters wake before dawn, gather clay from riverbeds, knead it with water and intention, and breathe life into every piece on the wheel. This is not manufacturing. This is meditation. This is mitti ka kaam.

Our Promise

Why MudHub?

We don't just sell pottery — we preserve a legacy

100% Eco-Friendly

Made from natural clay and fired using traditional methods. Our pottery returns to the earth, leaving zero carbon footprint. No chemicals, no plastics — just pure mitti.

Purely Handmade

Every piece is shaped by hand on a traditional potter's wheel. No machines, no molds. Each creation carries the fingerprints and love of its maker.

Traditional Craftsmanship

Our techniques have been passed down through generations. The same ancient methods our ancestors used to create vessels that stood the test of time.

Health Benefits

Clay vessels naturally cool water, add essential minerals, and are alkaline in nature. Drinking from mitti improves metabolism and boosts immunity.
